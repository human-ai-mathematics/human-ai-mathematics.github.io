Human–AI Mathematics

What a framework for mathematics done with AI has to do, what already exists, and a modest proposal for one part of it.

This site is meant to be temporary. Many groups are now building tools, registries, venues and norms for mathematics with AI, and this work makes most sense as part of a larger initiative or collaboration. When one exists that this work can join, the site will point there. Until then, feedback is welcome: start a discussion or open an issue on GitHub, or write to nicolas.brosse [at] ensae.fr.

AI systems now produce mathematics in quantity: proofs of open problems, very large formal libraries, bulk releases of manuscripts. In a few months the mathematical community has answered with a large number of projects, each addressing one part of the problem: problem registries, formal libraries, checkers, agent harnesses, deposit and review venues, disclosure rules. What is missing is a view of the whole: what a framework has to do, which parts are well served, where efforts overlap, and where nothing exists yet.

Why this project

We started with the Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits conjecture as a testbed. In October 2026 it was proved three times: three teams, each working with frontier AI models, reached independent proofs within days of one another. The effort was spent three times over, and almost none of it was shared along the way: which routes each team tried, which obstacles stopped them, which ideas they set aside.

That convinced us that research with AI needs shared infrastructure more than one more isolated tool, and that before proposing anything, one should look carefully at what already exists.

The landscape

A unified view of what a framework for mathematics with AI has to do, and a map of what exists against it: about 150 projects, from problem registries and formal libraries to research records, certification, venues and norms. With its sources and its date, since the field changes weekly.

Our proposal

Conjecture search

A modest proposal for one part of the landscape: keeping the record of a sustained search on one hard statement, by humans and AI agents, with every status certified by an independent review or a named person. Its principles, how it compares with similar projects, the KLS manuscript it produced, and the template to start a new search.
