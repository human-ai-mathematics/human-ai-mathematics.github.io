In October 2026 it was proved three times: three teams, each working with frontier AI models, reached independent proofs within days of one another. The effort was spent three times over, and almost none of it was shared along the way: which routes each team tried, which obstacles stopped them, which ideas they set aside.

That convinced us that research with AI needs shared infrastructure more than one more isolated tool, and that before proposing anything, one should look carefully at what already exists.

### [The landscape](landscape/)

A unified view of what a framework for mathematics with AI has to do, and a map of what exists against it: about 150 projects, from problem registries and formal libraries to research records, certification, venues and norms. With its sources and its date, since the field changes weekly.

### [Conjecture search](conjecture-search/)

A modest proposal for one part of the landscape: keeping the record of a sustained search on one hard statement, by humans and AI agents, with every status certified by an independent review or a named person. Its principles, how it compares with similar projects, the KLS manuscript it produced, and the template to start a new search.
